# Alert Candidate Review Packet Contract

## Purpose

Define the packet used to consider a ticker for research or high-attention alert coverage.

Required fields: ticker, research question, timeframe, thesis, source lineage, evidence date, freshness, confidence, catalyst, principal risks, alert condition, invalidation logic, uncertainty, and recommended review state.

Possible outcomes are admit to research coverage, admit to high-attention monitoring, monitor only, suppress pending evidence, or reject. Admission never grants action authority and never creates account or execution state.
