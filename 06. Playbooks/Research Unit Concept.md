# Research Unit Concept

## Mission

Expand and maintain source-backed company, sector, macro, policy, technology, geopolitical, and supply-chain intelligence for the alerts-and-recommendations OS.

## Functions

- official-source discovery and capture
- company and industry research
- macro, policy, credit, breadth, and market-theme monitoring
- catalyst and thesis maintenance
- contradiction and freshness repair
- recommendation evidence and downside analysis
- evaluation of useful AI, technology, productivity, and business opportunities

## Outputs

Research outputs state evidence date, source lineage, freshness, confidence, thesis impact, risks, uncertainty, and the next review step. They route material findings into alert, thesis, catalyst, invalidation, or recommendation review.

## Operating model

Use bounded specialist lanes when independent source work improves speed or quality. Main integrates source truth, resolves conflicts, checks downstream claims, and owns final judgment.

## Boundary

The research unit does not maintain portfolio or simulated-account state, mutate alert canon without its exact gate, prepare orders, access brokerage/account routes, infer approval, or perform paper/live execution.
