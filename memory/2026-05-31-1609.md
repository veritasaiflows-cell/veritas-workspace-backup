- Faster linting of artifacts.
- Faster SQL/cache/schema validation.

- Faster detection of missing source fields.

- reading new SEC filings
- deciding whether ETN, VRT, NVDA, CME, etc. are good entries
- reconciling thesis vs price vs band vs catalyst
- generating high-quality investment judgment
- source-opening real evidence
- owner-gated capital-deployment reasoning
