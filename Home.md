## What Matters Now

- Portfolio and finance judgment belongs in the canonical human notes.
- Generated JSON, validation packets, and proof artifacts belong in `tmp/`, `state/`, or `data/state-history/`.
- PDFs, Excel workbooks, HTML exports, and CSV deliverables belong in `10. Deliverables/`.
- Dashboards summarize; they do not overrule source notes, validators, or owner approval gates.
- PM surfaces show proof, queues, source health, and blockers; Randall-facing surfaces should show what matters for decisions.

## Review Path

The numbered folders define the default vault review order.

1. `01. Dashboards/` - quick orientation and executive summaries
2. `02. Markets/` - macro regime and market focus
3. `03. Portfolio/` - portfolio posture, model portfolio, and execution board state
4. `04. Research/` - thesis notes and coverage work
5. `05. Intelligence/` - thesis ranking, weekly briefs, and event intelligence
6. `06. Playbooks/` - operating methods, workflows, and protocols
7. `07. Risk/` - constraints, policies, and risk rules
8. `08. Audits/` - audits, health checks, and cleanup history
9. `09. Archive/` - retired but worth-keeping material; archive mutation remains separately gated
10. `10. Deliverables/` - human-facing PDFs, workbooks, HTML views, and CSV exports
