## Results summary

NVIDIA delivered a very strong print.

- **Revenue:** $81.6B, up **20% QoQ** and **85% YoY**.
- **Data Center revenue:** $75.2B, up **21% QoQ** and **92% YoY**.
- **GAAP EPS:** $2.39, up **36% QoQ** and **214% YoY**.
- **Non-GAAP EPS:** $1.87, up **18% QoQ** and **140% YoY**.
- **GAAP / non-GAAP gross margin:** 74.9% / 75.0%.
- **Q2 FY2027 revenue guide:** $91.0B ±2%.
- **China caveat:** Q2 outlook assumes **no Data Center compute revenue from China**.
- **Capital return:** additional $80.0B share-repurchase authorization and quarterly dividend increase from $0.01 to $0.25.
