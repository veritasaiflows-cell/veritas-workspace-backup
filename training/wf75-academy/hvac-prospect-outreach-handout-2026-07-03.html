Current Status

What exists

20 public-source prospects, a first 5 batch, QA-reviewed internal outreach packet, and a simulation lab.

What this enables

Training, rehearsal, owner approval-card prep, and safer manual outreach planning.

What it does not authorize

No outreach, sending, calling, SMS, CRM import, payment links, customer data, or production access.

Operating Flow

Offer Frame

Plain-English Offer

A fixed-scope 5-day review of one intake or callback workflow. It returns a practical map and improvement checklist without system access, customer data, production changes, or outcome guarantees.

Do Not Sell This As

  • guaranteed revenue or booked jobs
  • live automation build
  • CRM migration
  • text automation
  • call recording setup
  • emergency dispatch coverage
  • legal, compliance, or security certification
