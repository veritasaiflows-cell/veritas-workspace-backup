# Content Pivot Phase Plan

> Archived note. This file belongs to a retired operating branch. Preserve it as historical context, not current doctrine. Current live identity and operating rules are defined by SOUL.md, IDENTITY.md, AGENTS.md, MEMORY.md, and FINANCE_SOUL.MD.

## Goal

Use content to build confidence, sharpen message-market fit, gather audience signals, and create a path toward monetization without losing contact with real-world demand.

## Phase 1. Positioning and market learning

### Objective
Define the content angle clearly enough that it does not collapse into generic productivity noise.

### Outputs
- clear one-line positioning
- 3 content pillars
- pain-language bank from market research
- visible examples of what is already working in the niche

### Actions
1. Review [[07. Audits/Content Model Trend Scan - 2026-04-11]]
2. Decide the one-line positioning
3. Lock the 3 content pillars
4. Build a bank of audience pain statements and hook language

## Phase 2. Short-form content production

### Objective
Start publishing short-form content to get repetitions, confidence, and audience signal.

### Outputs
- first 20 short-form content ideas
- first 10 draft hooks
- first 5 scripts
- simple recording workflow

### Actions
1. Generate 20 content ideas from the pillars
2. Sort them by strongest pain and clearest hook
3. Draft 5 short videos first
4. Record and publish consistently instead of overproducing

## Phase 3. Signal and feedback loop

### Objective
Use audience response to sharpen the message instead of guessing forever.

### Outputs
- list of best-performing hooks
- repeated comment themes
- repeated pain language
- clearer audience fit

### Actions
1. Track what topics get attention
2. Track what phrasing gets comments or saves
3. Note where people say "that is me" or ask for help
4. Kill weak angles quickly

## Phase 4. Long-form authority building

### Objective
Deepen trust and show real thinking, not just short-form snippets.

### Outputs
- first long-form content topics
- breakdowns of prioritization systems and execution logic
- stronger authority around the niche

### Actions
1. Convert the strongest short-form topics into longer explanations
2. Use long-form to teach frameworks, not just tips
3. Build deeper trust through useful explanation and examples

## Phase 5. Monetization bridge

### Objective
Create a path from content attention to real business value.

### Outputs
- soft CTA to direct help
- lightweight offer path
- simple lead capture or DM path
- clearer monetization ladder

### Actions
1. Add a soft path like "DM me if you want help applying this"
2. Watch for repeated questions and pain patterns
3. Reintroduce service carefully when confidence and proof improve
4. Avoid building products before demand is clearer

## Guardrails

- Do not let content become avoidance of market reality.
- Do not talk so broadly that the niche disappears.
- Do not overproduce before publishing.
- Do not confuse views with business traction.
- Do not abandon real-world learning from direct human problems.

## Immediate next actions

1. Finalize the one-line positioning
2. Finalize the 3 content pillars
3. Generate the first 20 short-form content ideas
4. Choose the minimum viable publishing rhythm
