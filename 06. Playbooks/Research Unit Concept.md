# Research Unit Concept

## Purpose

Define the thin future concept of a Veritas research unit without prematurely turning it into a bloated operating structure.

This note exists to answer one question:

**At what point does coverage expansion justify a dedicated coordinated research layer instead of one-off contractor handoffs?**

## Current judgment

A research unit is **not yet required as a formal operating body**.

It is worth planning for now because the likely future pressure is real:
- broader portfolio coverage
- broader macro sleeve coverage
- more recurring post-earnings interpretation
- more thesis maintenance across a wider watch universe
- more need to separate implementation labor from research maintenance

But the current system still needs:
- lane semantics
- trust-state coherence
- consistency gating
- publication hardening

If those are not clean first, a research unit will just scale confusion.

## What the research unit is for

The research unit would exist to expand and maintain coverage in a disciplined way across:
- portfolio candidates
- current tracked names
- watch-only research names
- macro sleeves and regime-sensitive instruments
- recurring catalysts such as earnings, policy events, and macro releases

It should increase:
- coverage breadth
- maintenance reliability
- continuity across many names
- separation between initiation, maintenance, and portfolio decision support

It should not become a vague bucket for “more work.”

## What the research unit is not

It is not:
- a replacement for Veritas
- a freeform swarm of contractors
- a justification for adding names without lane semantics
- a second conflicting truth layer
- a permission slip for auto-rewriting canonical notes

Veritas remains:
- operating principal
- final integrator
- continuity owner
- trust and judgment owner

## Likely future shape

### Veritas
Owns:
- operating doctrine
- final integration
- portfolio decision support
- trust standards
- publication standards
- contractor orchestration

### Independent contractors
Own:
- bounded implementation passes
- bounded audit/review passes
- explicit phase-based workstreams
- reversible code/plumbing changes

### Research unit
Would eventually own:
- coverage initiation workflows
- thesis maintenance queue
- macro maintenance queue
- post-earnings maintenance queue
- watch-universe expansion queue
- evidence refresh expectations by lane

## Preconditions before formalizing it

Do not formalize the research unit until most of these are true:
- universe and lane semantics are stable
- trust propagation is coherent across dashboard/workbook/publication
- consistency gates fail loudly instead of allowing silent drift
- canonical note ownership is clearer
- recurring coverage-expansion demand is real, not hypothetical
- there is enough repeated work to justify named queues and ownership lanes

## Triggers that would justify formalization

Formalize a research unit when one or more of these become persistent:

### 1. Coverage breadth pressure
- the machine-tracked universe expands beyond the current manageable set
- multiple new names need ongoing maintenance rather than one-time research

### 2. Macro depth pressure
- macro coverage expands into more sleeves, instruments, or recurring scenario boards
- policy, rates, inflation, energy, credit, or breadth maintenance begins competing materially with equity maintenance

### 3. Repeated initiation pressure
- new thesis initiations become frequent enough that they need a repeatable intake path
- one-off deep dives are no longer enough

### 4. Recurring maintenance pressure
- post-earnings interpretation, thesis refreshes, and stale-note repair become a standing workload rather than occasional cleanup

### 5. Ownership pressure
- contractor handoffs start repeating the same role splits over and over
- the system clearly wants dedicated queues instead of ad hoc assignments

## Suggested future lanes inside a research unit

If formalized later, the unit should probably separate work into lanes such as:
- **Coverage initiation** — start new names, decide whether they belong in the machine universe
- **Thesis maintenance** — keep active names current
- **Macro maintenance** — keep regime, policy, credit, breadth, and sleeve-level context current
- **Catalyst maintenance** — earnings, event windows, post-event interpretation
- **Portfolio decision support** — promotion of relevant findings into portfolio/trigger/deployment layers

These are conceptual lanes, not a staffing chart.

## Minimum artifacts a research unit would need

Before a research unit is real, these artifacts should exist:
- coverage lanes and admission rules
- refresh expectations by lane
- evidence standards by lane
- queue definitions for new coverage vs maintenance
- promotion rules from research lane -> machine-tracked lane -> execution lane
- trust rules for how research artifacts affect surfaces and notes

## Recommended near-term posture

For now:
- use ICs for bounded implementation and audit work
- keep research-unit thinking conceptual
- observe repeated workload pressure
- record which future coverage expansions are truly needed
- avoid formalizing a separate unit until the OS can support it cleanly

## Best next step

The next step is **not** to create a research-unit project.
The next step is to finish:
1. universe/lane normalization
2. trust propagation
3. consistency gating
4. publication hardening

Then watch where the next real pressure appears:
- more portfolio names
- more macro sleeve coverage
- more recurring deep dives
- more post-earnings maintenance

When that pressure is visible, formalize a thin research-unit workflow note rather than improvising it in chat.

## Trigger question for future review

Ask this again when one of the following becomes true:
- we intentionally expand the machine-tracked universe meaningfully
- macro coverage needs dedicated standing maintenance beyond current cadence
- IC handoffs for research and maintenance become common enough to deserve separate ownership
- the current operating loop starts dropping important coverage because breadth has outgrown the present structure
