## Core rules

- Capital preservation comes first.
- Never recommend a position without a defined risk envelope.
- Every serious recommendation should state the main downside path.
- Avoid obscure, illiquid, or unregistered instruments.
- Treat leverage, options, and speculative crypto as elevated-risk domains.

## Sizing rules

- Tier 1: 8% to 12% normally
- Tier 2: 4% to 7%
- Tier 3: 1% to 3%
- Normal max single position: 15%
- Stretch max single position: 20% to 25% only for exceptional high-conviction cases after explicit review
- Max single sector: 25% to 35%
- Default speculative sleeve target: up to 5% total
- Do not exceed 10% total in the speculative sleeve without an explicit written exception

## Escalation triggers

- Portfolio drawdown greater than 6% to 8% from recent high
- Thesis impairment with a 15% or worse move against the position and no improving fundamentals
- Major macro regime change
- Circuit breaker, trading halt, or abnormal volatility event
- Any recommendation involving leverage or options
- Any proposal to exceed the normal 15% single-position limit
- Any draft posture or recommendation that exceeds the normal sector cap, exceeds the speculative-sleeve cap, or materially over-stacks one correlated sleeve
- Any plan to take stretch size, add above normal size, or materially increase exposure inside a major catalyst window (earnings, FOMC, CPI, or equivalent) without explicit event-risk justification

## Review standard

When reviewing a position, ask:
- Is the thesis intact?
- Is the position sized appropriately for conviction?
- Has the risk-reward improved or worsened?
- What invalidates the idea?
- What is the opportunity cost of holding?
- Am I treating several related names as separate ideas when they are really one crowded sleeve?
- Is a catalyst window making the current size or timing less attractive even if the chart still looks valid?
