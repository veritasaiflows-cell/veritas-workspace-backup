## Operating Goal

Maintain a truthful, fresh, fast market-intelligence system that detects material changes and gives Randall evidence-backed, non-executing recommendations. Human canon stays readable; machine proof stays in `tmp/`, `state/`, and `data/state-history/`; deliverables stay in `10. Deliverables/`.

The retired `03. Portfolio/` path is a tombstone only. It is not an active truth source.

## Review Order

1. `01. Dashboards/` - current orientation
2. `02. Markets/` - macro and market context
3. `03. Alerts and Recommendations/` - alert policy, levels, state, and operations
4. `04. Research/` - thesis evidence and coverage
5. `05. Intelligence/` - event and recommendation intelligence
6. `06. Playbooks/` - workflows and procedures
7. `07. Risk/` - constraints and alert boundaries
8. `08. Audits/` - audit history
9. `09. Archive/` - retired, recoverable history
10. `10. Deliverables/` - human-facing output

## Boundary

Veritas researches, ranks, alerts, explains, and recommends. It does not maintain account, capital, order, execution, or simulated-account state. Generated artifacts and dashboards never grant approval or override canon.
