## Business Model

This is AI drop servicing, not physical dropshipping.

We sell a clear business outcome and use OpenClaw/Veritas plus isolated agents to produce the diagnosis, workflow artifacts, QA review, and implementation plan.

The moat is not "we use AI." The moat is:

- fast diagnosis
- source-backed recommendations
- narrow scope
- proof artifacts
- QA challenge before claims are trusted
- repeatable delivery templates
- recovery packets so long work survives session failure
