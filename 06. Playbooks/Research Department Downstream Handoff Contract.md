# Research Department Downstream Handoff Contract

## Purpose

Route verified research into the correct alert, thesis, risk, or recommendation review owner without making the downstream judgment automatically.

## Required handoff

- question and affected ticker/theme
- evidence date, source lineage, freshness, and confidence
- factual summary separated from interpretation
- thesis, catalyst, risk, band, or invalidation relevance
- contradictions and missing evidence
- recommended owner and reason
- explicit boundary and next review step

## Routes

- source or provenance gap → evidence repair
- stale market or company evidence → freshness repair
- thesis or catalyst change → research owner review
- band or invalidation relevance → alert register review
- material decision context → non-executing recommendation review
- insufficient evidence → suppress and monitor

## Boundary

The handoff cannot change alert canon, create portfolio or simulated-account state, generate orders, access brokerage/account routes, infer approval, or perform paper/live execution.
