1. **Research intake and promotion judgment exist.** WF25 created the research queue, admission object, promotion/demotion object, and downstream handoff contract. That is the right base for market-intelligence autonomy. fileciteturn2file0

## Key gaps preventing more autonomy

### 1. Fresh-intelligence automation is still too manual

WF26 explicitly says the lane proved useful but not enough for standalone cron or wider autonomy. That is safe, but it means your agent may still miss market-moving events unless a human triggers review. fileciteturn2file1

**Recommendation:** create a new bounded workflow: **WF41 — Market Intelligence Event Intake and Materiality Router**.

It should run automatically, but only as a **read-only packet generator**. It should not update thesis, promote names, or change deployment state.

Output should be:

```json
{
  "event_id": "...",
  "ticker_or_macro_sleeve": "...",
  "source_tier": "tier_1 | tier_2 | unresolved",
  "event_type": "earnings | guidance | macro | geopolitical | rates | sector | management | risk",
  "materiality_score": 0-5,
  "thesis_field_impacted": "...",
  "portfolio_surface_impacted": "...",
  "recommended_route": "no_route | weekly_review | thesis_review | promotion_review | risk_review | deployment_review",
  "urgency": "today | this_week | monitor",
  "blocked_reason": "...",
  "owner_review_required": true
}
```

### 2. Capital deployment lacks a final “recommendation packet” layer

WF38 can generate candidate packets and promotion review checks, but the system still needs one narrow output that answers:

**“Should Randall consider deploying capital now, waiting, or rejecting the setup?”**

The agent should not execute, but it should be able to produce a structured recommendation.

**Recommendation:** add a **Capital Deployment Recommendation Object** downstream of WF38.

It should require:

This gives your agent more autonomy in **decision preparation**, without crossing into autonomous execution.

## Recommended autonomy ladder

Use this as the operating model.

| Level | Agent may do | Agent may not do | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Level 1 — Read-only monitor | Fetch/check approved sources, validate artifacts, flag drift | Mutate notes, change states, trade | Ready after WF40 stable |
| Level 2 — Packet generator | Create event packets, candidate packets, brief packets, risk packets | Promote, size, deploy | Mostly ready |
| Level 3 — Recommendation engine | Rank opportunities, propose deployment candidates, explain bull/bear/risk | Execute trades or silently update canon | Next target |
| Level 4 — Owner-approved mutation | Apply approved band updates or owner-approved note changes | Change thesis/deployment without approval | Limited; only exact-gated cases |
| Level 5 — Execution automation | Place trades, rebalance, execute deployment | Not recommended for this system now | Blocked |

Your near-term target should be **Level 3**, not Level 5.

## Final judgment

Your workflows are **not overbuilt**; they are building the right control system. The issue is that they currently emphasize safety, provenance, and truth boundaries more than autonomous market coverage. The next improvement is to let the agent act as a **daily market-intelligence chief of staff**:

- watch the right sources,
- detect material events,
- route them into the right review object,
- rank opportunities,
- prepare deployment recommendations,
- and stop at owner approval.

That gives you much more autonomy in market intelligence and capital-deployment decision making without creating an unsafe black-box trading system.
