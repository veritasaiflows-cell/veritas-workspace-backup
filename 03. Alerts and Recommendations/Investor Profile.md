# Investor Profile

## Purpose

Calibrate finance alerts and recommendations to Randall's objectives without maintaining account or portfolio state.

## Decision Context

- Primary orientation: long term.
- Secondary lenses: tactical, speculative, and income opportunities, expressed as recommendation labels rather than system-owned sleeves.
- Time to retirement: approximately 25 years.
- Objective: build long-term wealth using a reliable early-warning and recommendation system.
- Volatility tolerance when a thesis remains intact: medium-high.
- Escalate for a full risk review when evidence points to roughly a 6% to 8% drawdown or a material thesis break.
- Treat roughly 15% to 20% drawdown risk as a high-severity alert, not as permission to act.

## Alert Preferences

- Lead with truth, evidence, freshness, uncertainty, and downside.
- Alert on material band entry, invalidation-threshold breach, thesis change, catalyst surprise, macro-regime shift, and freshness decay.
- Suppress ordinary noise and duplicate alerts.
- Distinguish company quality from timing quality.
- For a material recommendation, state timeframe, confidence, evidence date, thesis, bull/base/bear cases, risks, relevant band, invalidation logic, and Randall's next decision.

## Cadence

- Daily: evidence, quote, trigger, and source-freshness checks.
- Weekly: thesis, band, invalidation, and recommendation review.
- Event-driven: material earnings, company, sector, policy, or macro change.

## Instruments in Scope

- Public stocks and liquid ETFs.
- Macro-linked rates, energy, defense, and commodity intelligence.
- Thematic groups such as AI infrastructure and geopolitical change.

## Authority Boundary

The system may produce non-executing recommendations. Randall owns thesis acceptance, invalidation judgment, capital, orders, account actions, and execution.

This profile is not a holdings record and does not authorize or maintain sleeves, positions, allocations, weights, sizing, tranches, cash, rebalancing, or simulated account state.
