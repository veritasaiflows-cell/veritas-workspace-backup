# Research Automation Source Bundle Contract

## Purpose

Define a bounded, source-backed bundle for research, alert review, and non-executing recommendations.

## Bundle contents

- question, ticker, sector, or market theme
- evidence window and capture time
- approved primary and secondary sources
- exact URLs, publication dates, and source tiers
- normalized facts separated from interpretation
- contradictions, missing evidence, and unresolved identity
- thesis, catalyst, risk, band, or invalidation relevance
- freshness and confidence
- deterministic bundle hash

## Coverage review

The coverage check asks whether the bundle omits a material company filing, official release, macro source, regulator, policy source, industry source, or credible geopolitical/supply source. Market-theme coverage is evidence organization only and never a system-owned finance category.

## Promotion rules

A bundle may support:

- research-note review
- thesis or catalyst review
- alert-state review
- freshness repair
- a non-executing recommendation

It may not directly rewrite canon, create finance action-state, infer approval, or reach brokerage/account/order/execution routes. Conflicted or stale evidence must lower confidence or suppress the recommendation.

## Output boundary

Bundles live in a clearly generated proof location and remain subordinate to active canon and exact source truth.
