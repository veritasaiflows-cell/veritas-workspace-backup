# Editing Principles for Short-Form

> Archived note. This file belongs to a retired operating branch. Preserve it as historical context, not current doctrine. Current live identity and operating rules are defined by SOUL.md, IDENTITY.md, AGENTS.md, MEMORY.md, and FINANCE_SOUL.MD.

## Purpose

This document defines how editing should serve short-form performance.

Editing is not there to show off.
It is there to improve clarity, pacing, and retention.

## Core rule

Edit to keep attention.
Do not edit to impress yourself.

## Main editing goals

1. remove dead space
2. improve pacing
3. increase clarity
4. make the video easier to watch
5. reinforce the main point

## Editing principles

### 1. Cut aggressively where needed
If a pause, repeat, filler phrase, or weak sentence does not help, cut it.

### 2. Keep the visual rhythm alive
The viewer should feel movement and progression.
That does not mean random flashy edits.
It means the video should never feel stagnant for too long.

### 3. Use captions as support
Captions help retention and accessibility.
But bad captions create clutter.

Use captions that are:
- readable
- clean
- synchronized well
- visually consistent

### 4. Do not overload effects
Too many transitions, animations, zooms, or stickers make beginner content feel cheaper, not better.

Use effects only when they improve communication.

### 5. Preserve natural energy
Do not edit so tightly that the speaker sounds robotic.
Clean is good.
Artificial is not.

### 6. Make the key point easy to catch
Use pacing, cuts, text emphasis, or framing changes to help the strongest idea land.

### 7. Optimize for mobile viewing
Short-form content is usually watched vertically and quickly.
So text must be readable, framing must be clear, and visual clutter must stay low.

## Beginner editing priorities

For now, focus on:
- cutting filler
- tightening pauses
- adding readable captions
- improving pacing
- exporting clean vertical videos

## Things to avoid early

- overdesigned motion graphics
- complicated editing stacks
- spending hours polishing weak scripts
- using effects to hide weak delivery
- adding visual noise just to look “high effort”

## Truth

Editing can improve a strong video.
It cannot rescue a weak hook, weak idea, or weak delivery.

Do not expect editing to do the job of strategy.
