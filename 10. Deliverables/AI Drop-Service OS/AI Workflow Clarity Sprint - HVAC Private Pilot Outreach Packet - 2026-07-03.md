## Discovery Fit Gate

A prospect is worth a call only if they confirm at least one of these:
- Calls or form leads sometimes wait for callback.
- After-hours or emergency requests create handoff issues.
- The owner cannot easily see what happened between inquiry and callback.
- Booking/scheduling is split across phone, form, vendor, or manual follow-up.
- They want a simple workflow map before buying tools.

Disqualify if:
- They want guaranteed revenue or booked-job improvement.
- They require emergency-response claims.
- They ask for customer data ingestion before trust boundaries are set.
- They require us to log into their systems during the pilot.
- They want a full implementation before diagnosis.
