## Suggested pilot framing

I am offering a limited pilot for ambitious investors, founders, and operators who feel overloaded by ideas, information, and possible directions.

The goal is to build a structured opportunity operating system that helps you rank opportunities, focus your attention, and move into clearer execution.

## What the pilot includes

- intake and diagnosis
- opportunity pipeline setup
- scoring and prioritization framework
- dashboard structure
- weekly review system
- ranked current opportunities
- first execution recommendations

## Suggested pilot pricing logic

Pick one of these approaches:

1. Free pilot for one ideal user in exchange for full feedback and testimonial
2. Discounted founding-client rate
3. Full price if the client is clearly ideal and already convinced

## Recommendation

Start with either:
- one discounted founding client
- or one strong free pilot if the learning value is high enough

Do not run too many free pilots.

## Success standard

A successful pilot should produce:
- obvious clarity improvement
- a real ranked pipeline
- a strong client reaction
- clearer language for the offer
- evidence that this is worth repeating
