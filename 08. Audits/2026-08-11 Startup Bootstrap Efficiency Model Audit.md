## Executive Verdict

The prior state was not sufficient. The recent execution-efficiency rules existed in implementation artifacts, but cold sessions, persistent helpers, wiki retrieval, and several operating procedures could still receive stale or contradictory doctrine. Startup/status also implied a successful Main/Sol route without authoritative actual-route evidence. That made the efficiency work easy to bypass and made some startup truth optimistic.

The repair makes `veritas.execution_efficiency_policy.v1` the reusable policy owner in `scripts/project_implementation_router.py` and projects that contract into startup, future-session pickup, status, wiki synthesis/validation, generated persistent-agent bootstraps, skills, and procedures. New sessions now receive the policy through their normal front doors instead of depending on daily memory recall.

## Canonical Efficiency Contract

1. Run deterministic/model-free commands and proof first.
2. Use Codex-native only when explicitly allowed and bounded: read-only Terra/low or a one-file leased implementation on Terra/medium.
3. Use Main/Sol only for an explicit quick fix, final integration, or authority-sensitive judgment.
4. Otherwise use a persistent Terra helper only when a fresh strict transport-capability proof exists; missing proof blocks dispatch rather than silently falling back to Main.
5. Freeze helper handoffs at no more than 6 files, 120,000 bytes, and about 30,000 estimated context tokens; preserve base path, file hashes, snapshot identity, attempt/retry metadata, and the 90-second incident update.
6. Close out against actual backend, model, and effort. Missing actual evidence remains unavailable; mismatches fail closed.
7. Optimize for accepted outcomes: first-pass acceptance, uncached tokens, gross replay, elapsed time, retry tax, and escaped defects.
8. Compare like-for-like cohorts only. A route needs at least 10 comparable Main-accepted jobs for review; automatic ranking or promotion remains disabled.

5. **Compact remaining boot/control surfaces in separate lanes.** Thin curated memory and live workflow summaries only with provenance, archive mapping, retrieval proof, and rollback; do not turn compaction into a broad cleanup.
6. **Use validator timing evidence.** Recommend the smallest validation bundle that historically catches the task class's defects; never let timing alone waive authority or correctness gates.
7. **Cap ordinary rework.** After one bounded builder repair and one fresh QA rerun, return unresolved findings to Main for scope reduction or an explicit decision instead of launching an unbounded helper chain.
