# Research Coverage Admission Review Object

## Purpose

Decide whether a company, instrument, sector, or market theme deserves ongoing research and alert attention.

## Required review fields

- candidate and research question
- evidence date, source lineage, and confidence
- business or market relevance
- thesis, catalyst, and primary risks
- timeframe and recommendation risk label
- comparison with existing research coverage
- expected alert or learning value
- data availability and freshness cost
- unresolved evidence gaps

## Outcomes

- `admit_active_research`
- `admit_monitor_only`
- `one_time_review`
- `defer_for_evidence`
- `reject_low_value`

Admission creates research attention only. It does not create holdings, positions, allocations, weights, sizing, orders, accounts, or execution authority.
