# AI Workflow Clarity Sprint

**A fixed-scope, 5-day workflow review for HVAC operators — no software install, no system access, no customer data required.**

## The problem

Most HVAC shops already advertise fast response, same-day service, and easy scheduling. But between the missed call, the voicemail, the web form, and the callback, inbound opportunities can stall — and it's hard to see where. Nobody owns the 15-minute response window, callback ownership is fuzzy, and the owner can't easily see what happened between inquiry and booking.

## What the Sprint does

Over 5 business days, we map **one workflow** — missed-call recovery and callback handoff — from the moment an inquiry arrives to the moment it's booked, followed up, or closed out. You get a clear picture of where requests stall and a practical, ranked plan to fix it.

## What you receive

1. **Current-state workflow map** — how missed calls and callbacks actually flow today
2. **Missed-opportunity and handoff-risk summary** — where inquiries stall or go dark
3. **Structured intake checklist** — so every inquiry is captured the same way
4. **Callback and handoff workflow map** — clear ownership from inquiry to dispatch
5. **Intake-to-dispatch handoff checklist**
6. **Ranked improvement plan** — top 5 fixes scored by value, effort, and risk
7. **Proof-of-concept prompt/spec pack** — ready-to-use AI templates for the narrow workflow
8. **30-day implementation backlog** — what to do next, in order
9. **Closeout walkthrough** — a 30-minute call to review everything

Delivered as a PDF pack plus the walkthrough call.

## What we need from you

- One kickoff conversation (about 30-45 minutes)
- A description of how inquiries arrive and get handled today
- Redacted or example-level detail only — **no logins, no credentials, no customer database, no call recordings**

## What this is not

- Not a software install or production system change
- Not a guarantee of revenue, bookings, or response times
- Not a legal, compliance, or emergency-response service
- Not a replacement for your office staff

It is a diagnostic: a clear map, a ranked plan, and practical templates you can act on with or without us.
