# Weekly Sector Opportunity Review Procedure

## Purpose

Run one clean weekly review to identify sector-level evidence changes and the one or two names most deserving of a fresh alert or non-executing recommendation review.

## When to use

- after the Sunday alerts-and-recommendations refresh;
- after a material regime change;
- after an earnings cluster materially changes the evidence set.

## Read first

- `02. Markets/Regime Scoring Matrix.md`
- `03. Alerts and Recommendations/Investor Profile.md`
- `03. Alerts and Recommendations/Alert Trigger Policy.md`
- `03. Alerts and Recommendations/Alert Bands and Invalidation Register.md`
- `07. Risk/Risk Rules.md`
- current macro, quote, freshness-controller, and weekly digest proofs.

## Procedure

1. Identify the strongest evidence-backed sector or theme change.
2. Separate market leadership, valuation, catalyst, and risk evidence from judgment.
3. Rank one or two candidates using source quality, evidence date, timeframe, freshness, confidence, thesis, base/bull/bear cases, and uncertainty.
4. For each candidate, check:
   - thesis and thesis breakers;
   - regime and sector context;
   - canonical alert-band and invalidation context;
   - catalyst timing;
   - source lineage and quote/session age;
   - suppression, no-chase, or monitor-only conditions.
5. If a material field is missing or stale, downgrade or suppress the recommendation and name the evidence repair needed.
6. Route the result to the weekly alert/recommendation digest. Any durable alert-canon correction requires its exact owner gate, numeric-preservation proof, validation, and audit.

## Operator output

Record:

- the sector/theme evidence change;
- the top one or two candidates;
- evidence date, freshness, confidence, and uncertainty;
- alert and recommendation-review state;
- thesis, risks, band/invalidation context, and blockers;
- Randall's decision point, if one exists.

## Stop lines

- no automatic promotion or finance-canon mutation;
- no maintained sleeves, exposure targets, holdings, allocations, weights, sizing, or simulated positions;
- no order package, execution route, brokerage/account action, or capital authority;
- no claim of currentness when the evidence or quote/session gate is stale.
