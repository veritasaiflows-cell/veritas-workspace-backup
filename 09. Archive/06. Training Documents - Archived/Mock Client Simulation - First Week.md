## Purpose

This document helps Randall practice what the first consulting sprint would feel like from the client side.

## Client question

"What is this going to look like after the first week is done?"

## Strong answer

After the first week, you should not feel like you received motivational advice or random productivity tips.

You should leave with a working operating system.

That means you would have:
- a structured opportunity pipeline
- a way to score and rank your current opportunities
- a dashboard that shows what matters now
- a weekly review system so the structure stays alive
- a clearer primary focus
- concrete next actions instead of a pile of vague ideas

## What the client would see

### 1. Opportunity Pipeline
A single place where opportunities are captured, classified, ranked, and tracked.

### 2. Scoring System
A simple but consistent way to judge what is worth attention and what should be paused or killed.

### 3. Dashboard
A view that shows:
- current primary focus
- top priorities
- active opportunities
- what needs attention next

### 4. Weekly Review
A repeatable rhythm for:
- checking current opportunities
- ranking them again if needed
- killing distractions
- choosing the next most important action

## What changes after the first week

The main change is not just organization.

The real change should be:
- less chaos
- fewer false priorities
- better decisions
- more confidence about what to work on next

## What this is not

It is not a guarantee of instant revenue.
It is not therapy.
It is not generic productivity coaching.
It is not a full software platform.

It is a decision and execution operating system.
