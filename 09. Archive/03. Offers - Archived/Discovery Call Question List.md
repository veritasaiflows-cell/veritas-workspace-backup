# Discovery Call Question List

> Archived note. This file belongs to a retired operating branch. Preserve it as historical context, not current doctrine. Current live identity and operating rules are defined by SOUL.md, IDENTITY.md, AGENTS.md, MEMORY.md, and FINANCE_SOUL.MD.

## Purpose

Use this call to diagnose the execution problem before prescribing a workflow, dashboard, or sprint.

Do not jump straight into solution mode.

## Core rule

You are trying to understand:
- what they are trying to do
- what keeps breaking
- what priorities are competing
- what a useful outcome would look like

## Questions

### 1. Current situation
- What are the main ideas, projects, or business priorities currently competing for your attention?
- Which of these feel most important right now?
- Which ones keep getting pushed, revisited, or left half-finished?

### 2. Pain and breakdowns
- Where does execution actually break down for you?
- Is the main problem too many ideas, weak prioritization, lack of visibility, poor follow-through, or something else?
- What have you already tried to get more organized?
- Why has that not fully solved the problem?

### 3. Current system
- How are you currently tracking ideas, priorities, and next steps?
- Do you already use notes, a task tool, spreadsheets, dashboards, or a project system?
- What part of the current system is useful, and what part is failing?

### 4. Desired outcome
- If this worked well, what would be different in the next 30 to 60 days?
- What decisions would become easier?
- What would clearer execution look like for you in real terms?

### 5. Decision criteria
- How do you currently decide what is worth doing now versus later?
- What criteria should be used to rank opportunities or projects?
- What kinds of work create the most value for you if you focus properly?

### 6. Constraints
- What time, energy, team, or business constraints matter here?
- Are there deadlines, dependencies, or outside pressures we need to account for?

### 7. Fit and urgency
- How urgent does this problem feel to you right now?
- If we solved this, what would the business or personal upside be?
- Does a focused setup sprint sound useful if it results in a clearer operating system and next-action path?

## What to capture during the call

Write down:
- active opportunities/projects
- recurring bottlenecks
- current tools/systems used
- desired outcomes
- ranking criteria
- urgency level
- likely fit for a pilot or sprint

## What success looks like

By the end of the call, you should be able to say clearly:
- what the real problem is
- why their current approach is failing
- what kind of engagement would actually help
