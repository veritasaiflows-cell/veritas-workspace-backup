## One-Sentence Offer

## Target Buyer Slice

Primary first slice: single-location residential HVAC operator with a small office/front-desk team, high phone reliance, and a manual or semi-manual missed-call/callback handoff flow.

Avoid for first pilot:

- Multi-location enterprise HVAC operators
- Regulated or insurance-heavy workflows
- Any shop requiring credentialed production-system access
- Any workflow involving call recording, SMS automation, or customer-data imports before separate privacy review
