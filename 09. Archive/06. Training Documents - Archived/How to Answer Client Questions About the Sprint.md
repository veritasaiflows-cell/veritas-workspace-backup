# How to Answer Client Questions About the Sprint

> Archived note. This file belongs to a retired operating branch. Preserve it as historical context, not current doctrine. Current live identity and operating rules are defined by SOUL.md, IDENTITY.md, AGENTS.md, MEMORY.md, and FINANCE_SOUL.MD.

## Purpose

This document helps Randall explain the consulting offer clearly and confidently.

## Question: "What will I actually have after the first week?"

Strong answer:

You will have a working operating system for handling your opportunities more clearly.
That includes a structured pipeline, a scoring method, a dashboard, a weekly review process, and a clearer sense of what deserves your attention now.

## Question: "What is the dashboard?"

Strong answer:

The dashboard is a simple operating view that shows what matters now. It keeps the client from getting lost in too many ideas or too many notes. It shows the active focus, the top priorities, and the next decisions that need to be made.

## Question: "What is the review system?"

Strong answer:

The review system is the weekly process that keeps the operating system alive. Without it, the system would decay into another pile of notes. The review process helps the client reassess opportunities, cut weak options, confirm the primary focus, and choose the next actions.

## Question: "What if I already use notes and task systems?"

Strong answer:

That is fine. This is not about replacing every tool. It is about creating a clearer decision structure so your ideas, opportunities, and focus are organized in a way that actually improves action.

## Question: "Is this software?"

Strong answer:

No. The first version is a structured consulting sprint that gives you a decision and execution operating system. If later we find that software is the real bottleneck, that can become a later layer.

## Core communication rule

Do not oversell AI.
Sell clarity, prioritization, and execution.
AI is part of the leverage, not the main promise.
