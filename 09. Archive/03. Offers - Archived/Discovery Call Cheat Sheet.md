# Discovery Call Cheat Sheet

> Archived note. This file belongs to a retired operating branch. Preserve it as historical context, not current doctrine. Current live identity and operating rules are defined by SOUL.md, IDENTITY.md, AGENTS.md, MEMORY.md, and FINANCE_SOUL.MD.

## Purpose

Use this during the live call.
Keep it visible.
Do not read it word for word like a script.

## Core rule

Diagnose first.
Do not jump into dashboards, AI, or solutions too early.

## 1. Open the call

Say something like:

I want to understand how you are currently managing ideas, priorities, and execution.
If there is a real fit, I will suggest a concrete next step instead of giving you vague advice.

## 2. Ask the key questions

### Situation
- What priorities, ideas, or projects are currently competing for your attention?
- Which ones matter most right now?
- Which ones keep getting delayed or revisited?

### Breakdown
- Where does execution actually break down?
- Is the problem too many ideas, weak prioritization, poor visibility, inconsistent follow-through, or something else?
- What have you tried already?
- Why has that not solved it?

### Current system
- How are you currently tracking ideas, priorities, and next steps?
- What is working in that system?
- What is failing?

### Desired result
- If this worked well, what would be different in the next 30 to 60 days?
- What would clearer execution actually look like?

### Criteria and constraints
- How should priorities really be ranked?
- What constraints matter: time, energy, people, deadlines?

## 3. What to capture

Write down:
- active projects or opportunities
- recurring bottlenecks
- current tools or systems
- desired outcomes
- ranking criteria
- urgency
- fit for a pilot or sprint

## 4. Reflect the diagnosis back

Say something like:

It sounds like the real problem is not a lack of ideas.
It is too many competing priorities without a reliable system for ranking and execution.

## 5. Frame the solution

Keep it simple:
- clearer opportunity capture
- prioritization framework
- dashboard for visibility
- weekly review rhythm
- next-action path

## 6. Propose the next step

Offer one concrete move:
- follow-up session
- pilot engagement
- 1-week setup sprint

Do not end with vague interest.

## 7. What you are actually selling

Not AI.
Not just a dashboard.

You are selling:
- clarity
- prioritization
- execution discipline
- a better operating system for action

## 8. Mistakes to avoid

- prescribing too early
- talking abstractly
- overselling AI
- ending without a next step

## 9. Good call outcome

A good first call ends with:
- a clear diagnosis
- a clear bottleneck
- a clear fit or no-fit
- a concrete next step
