# WF75 Academy Feedback Rubric

Use this after each micro-learning session. The goal is practical readiness, not memorization.

## Score Bands

| Score | Meaning | Standard |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | Not yet | Cannot explain the idea without major confusion or unsafe claims. |
| 1 | Basic recognition | Understands the concept when prompted, but cannot apply it cleanly. |
| 2 | Working grasp | Can apply the concept to a fake scenario with minor correction. |
| 3 | Ready for supervised use | Can explain, apply, and catch stop-line issues without major correction. |

## Daily Checks

1. Offer clarity: Can Randall explain what the service does in plain English?
2. Scope control: Can Randall say what the service does not do?
3. Workflow thinking: Can Randall identify trigger, bottleneck, owner, next action, and outcome?
4. Tool judgment: Can Randall avoid recommending Zapier/Make/CRM too early?
5. Safety/QA: Can Randall catch real-data, credential, outreach, implementation, ROI, legal/compliance, and certification overreach?
6. Explain-back: Can Randall explain the session in 90 seconds without reading?

## Feedback Loop

After each session, Veritas should give:

- score by category
- what Randall got right
- what needs correction
- one revised explanation Randall should practice
- one next-session adjustment

## Completion Standard

Randall completes the first 7-session Academy pass when:

- all daily sessions are attempted
- no category is below 2 on the final pass
- stop-line awareness is 3
- Randall can explain the manual SMB Workflow Clarity service without promising implementation, revenue, or compliance readiness
- Randall can walk through one fake scenario from intake to recommended next action

Boundary: internal training only. This rubric does not certify Randall, authorize customer outreach, permit real customer data, grant credential access, approve implementation, or allow external delivery.
