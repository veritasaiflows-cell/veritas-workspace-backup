# Opportunity Scoring Rubric

> Archived note. This file belongs to a retired operating branch. Preserve it as historical context, not current doctrine. Current live identity and operating rules are defined by SOUL.md, IDENTITY.md, AGENTS.md, MEMORY.md, and FINANCE_SOUL.MD.

Score each category from 1 to 10.

## Primary weighted factors

- **Reliability**: How likely is this to produce real value if executed well?
- **Upside**: How meaningful is the potential return or leverage?

## Secondary factors

- **Speed**: How quickly can this produce evidence, users, cash flow, or clear validation?
- **Capital efficiency**: How well does this fit moderate capital constraints?
- **Execution fit**: How well does this align with Randall's strengths in market analysis, investing, business, technology, and teaching?
- **Defensibility**: Can this become differentiated, durable, or hard to replace?
- **Reputation safety**: Does this align with responsible, durable, non-sloppy work?

## Scoring guidance

### Reliability
- 9-10: clear path, known demand, high confidence
- 7-8: strong thesis, some uncertainty
- 5-6: plausible but unproven
- 1-4: speculative or weakly grounded

### Upside
- 9-10: major cash flow, equity, or strategic leverage
- 7-8: meaningful upside if executed well
- 5-6: modest upside
- 1-4: low payoff or weak leverage

### Speed
- 9-10: can validate or monetize very quickly
- 7-8: can validate in weeks
- 5-6: medium path to proof
- 1-4: slow to prove or monetize

## Decision rule

An idea should not remain active just because it is interesting.

Promote ideas with:
- strong reliability
- meaningful upside
- a clear next action

Kill or pause ideas that:
- stay vague
- require too much speculative effort
- trigger overbuilding without action
