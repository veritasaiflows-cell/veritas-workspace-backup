## What BKNG is

Booking Holdings is a global online travel and services platform across Booking.com, Priceline, Agoda, KAYAK, and OpenTable. The company describes itself as serving travelers and partners in over 220 countries and territories, with a strategy centered on the Connected Trip, global reach, and use of AI / machine learning to improve traveler and partner value.

For portfolio construction, BKNG is cleaner than AMZN as a Consumer Discretionary diversifier because the exposure is travel / online travel services rather than a hybrid of discretionary retail, cloud, advertising, logistics, and AI infrastructure.

## Financial quality

### Revenue and margin structure

BKNG’s Q1 revenue was $5.532B versus $4.762B last year, while operating income rose to $1.271B from $1.062B. That implies operating income growth slightly ahead of revenue and confirms the platform’s margin durability.

The revenue mix remains useful:
- Merchant revenue: $3.698B, up from $2.918B.
- Agency revenue: $1.528B, slightly down from $1.564B.
- Advertising and other: $306M, up from $280M.

The merchant model growth supports scale and control but also raises payments, working-capital, and regulatory complexity. That is manageable, but it is not a no-risk mix shift.
