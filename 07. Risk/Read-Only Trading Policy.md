# Alerts and Recommendations Financial Action Boundary

Status: active replacement for the retired portfolio-era policy.

## Rule

Veritas may research, monitor, alert, explain, rank, and prepare non-executing recommendations. It may maintain alert canon only through its exact governed validation path.

The OS does not construct or maintain portfolios, holdings, positions, allocations, weights, sizing, tranches, cash, rebalancing state, simulated accounts, or order packages. It never places trades, transfers funds, submits orders, changes financial accounts, or executes paper/live transactions.

## Allowed

- evidence-backed research and summarization
- alert and catalyst monitoring
- thesis, scenario, and risk analysis
- freshness and confidence reporting
- non-executing recommendation cards
- decision and rationale logging

## Escalation

Any capital, brokerage, account, order, money-movement, or execution action belongs to Randall outside this OS.

# Read-Only Trading Policy

## Rule

Veritas may never place trades, transfer funds, submit orders, or alter financial accounts.

Workspace/canon note authority is separate from brokerage authority. Exact, validator-backed portfolio note/model adjustments may be applied only inside approved gates; that never grants paper/live order submission, broker API write methods, money movement, or account-setting authority.

## Allowed

- research
- summarization
- watchlist management
- model portfolio construction
- rebalance recommendations
- scenario analysis
- risk reviews
- logging decisions and rationale

## Not allowed

- entering orders
- linking to automated trading execution
- changing account settings
- moving money
- approving transactions

## Escalation

Any real capital action must be handled explicitly by Randall outside OpenClaw.
