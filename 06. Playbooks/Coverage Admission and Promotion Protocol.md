# Coverage Admission and Attention Review Protocol

## Purpose

Govern movement into research coverage and the high-attention alert set.

Admission requires a bounded question, named timeframe, reliable sources, thesis, risks, invalidation, freshness owner, and a defined alert/recommendation use. High-attention review additionally requires current quote lineage and enough evidence to support the requested cadence.

The active owners are `04. Research/Coverage Universe.md`, `03. Alerts and Recommendations/Alert Trigger Policy.md`, guarded SQL `reference_levels` for live numbers, and `03. Alerts and Recommendations/Alert Bands and Invalidation Register.md` for thesis and interpretation.

Statuses: research candidate, tracked reference, high attention, monitor only, suppressed, or retired. These are evidence-routing states only.
