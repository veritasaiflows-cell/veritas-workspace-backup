# Ticker Alert Lane Templates

## Research Record

- Ticker and company/theme
- Timeframe and research question
- Thesis and counter-thesis
- Evidence date, source lineage, freshness, and confidence
- Catalysts and principal risks
- Invalidation conditions

## Alert Record

- Reference low/high and invalidation threshold
- Latest quote and quote timestamp
- Level review date
- State: recommendation review, band entry, near band, no chase, invalidation alert, thesis change, catalyst alert, freshness decay, monitor only, or suppressed
- Uncertainty and next review

## Recommendation Record

- Base/bull/bear view
- Why now or why wait
- Evidence gaps
- Randall's decision point

These templates own no account or execution state.
