# Veritas Investment Intelligence Deck

## Purpose

Convert grounded market or company research into a scannable presentation. The deck packages evidence and non-executing recommendations; it does not create authority.

## Sources

Read the active alerts/recommendations canon, guarded SQL proof, current quote/freshness proof, relevant fundamental/macro/technical research, and current digest. Reuse validated visual assets when available. Preserve source dates and uncertainty.

## Default Slides

1. title, scope, timeframe, and as-of
2. executive conclusion and trust state
3. thesis and what changed
4. business/macro evidence
5. valuation or scenario context
6. technical alert and guarded threshold context
7. catalysts
8. base / bull / bear
9. risks, thesis breakers, and invalidation
10. non-executing recommendation and Randall's decision point
11. sources/methodology appendix when needed

## Recommendation States

Use Recommendation review, Band entry, Near band, No chase, Invalidation alert, Thesis change, Catalyst alert, Freshness decay, Monitor only, or Suppressed.

## Design Rules

One idea per slide. Lead with the conclusion. Use short bullets and visuals that explain evidence. Show dates, freshness, confidence, caveats, and source lineage. Never polish stale or conflicting evidence into certainty.

## Boundary

No system-owned holdings, positions, sleeves, allocations, weights, sizing, tranches, cash, rebalancing, simulated positions, order packages, account reads, or execution routes. Decks are review artifacts and never imply capital or execution approval.
