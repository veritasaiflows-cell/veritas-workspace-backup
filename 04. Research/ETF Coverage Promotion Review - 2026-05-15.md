## Individual ETF packets

### XLI — Industrial Select Sector SPDR
- **Role:** core industrial/cyclical sector monitor.
- **Why it matters:** captures broad industrial leadership, capex, onshoring, transport, defense/aerospace, and machinery without forcing single-name risk.
- **Portfolio fit:** useful benchmark against ETN, CAT, PH, GE, and broader industrial cyclicals.
- **Promotion case:** if industrial breadth improves and single-name setups are noisy, XLI can serve as the cleanest sector-level monitor or future sleeve candidate.
- **Risks:** cyclical earnings sensitivity, rate pressure, manufacturing slowdown, overlap with ETN/PH/CAT/GE research lanes.
- **Technical status:** underdefined pending ETF technical refresh.
- **Verdict:** **Promote to coverage monitoring.** Highest-priority ETF monitor.

### XLB — Materials Select Sector SPDR

- **Why it matters:** clean liquid proxy for Materials leadership while LIN/ECL/VMC/SHW/FCX work is unresolved.

- **Risks:** concentrated large-cap basket; chemical/gas/mining/cyclical exposure can diverge from LIN's quality profile.
- **Technical status:** underdefined pending ETF technical refresh.
- **Verdict:** **Promote to coverage monitoring.** Best Materials ETF monitor.
