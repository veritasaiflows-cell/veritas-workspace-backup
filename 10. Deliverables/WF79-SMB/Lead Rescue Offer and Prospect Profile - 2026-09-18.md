## The offer (one page)

**Lead Rescue Sprint** — for local service businesses losing leads to missed calls, buried web forms, and stale quotes.

Positioning: *We install a simple lead and follow-up operating system so marketing stops leaking through missed calls, stale leads, buried forms, and inconsistent follow-up.*

**What the customer gets** (3–5 business days, manual-first, sanitized samples only — no credentials, no system access):

1. **Lead-source and leak map** — where calls, forms, texts, quote requests enter and get lost
2. **Owner attention queue** — daily next-action list: lead status, stale-lead flags, callback priority, blocker reason
3. **Follow-up script/message pack** — callback, quote follow-up, appointment reminder, review/referral wording; human-approval-only
4. **Tool-stack gap notes** — process gaps and low-risk next steps before any automation
5. **Pilot recap + next-action plan** — what changed, what's still manual, what an automation phase would require

**Why it's different:** governed delivery. Audit trail on every action, owner approval gate on every outbound word, zero credentials required to start. Proof-bearing operations — not an agency retainer.

**Offer ladder (June 2026 pricing hypotheses, assumption-only, not market-validated):**
| Stage | What | Price hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| Lead Rescue Sprint | manual pilot above | $500–$1,500 fixed |
| Marketing Follow-Up Engine | automation build after manual packet proves fit | $1,500–$5,000 setup |
| Monthly Lead + Marketing Ops Packet | review-only recap, queue cleanup, improvement recommendations | $250–$1,000/mo |

**Pricing stop line (unchanged):** do not publish, quote, invoice, or promise pricing until Randall approves a specific pilot scope.

### The $10k/month math — honest version

June pricing hypotheses underprice the retainer. Best case at those ranges: 6 clients × $1,000/mo = $6k recurring + ~1 setup/mo at $2.5k ≈ **$8.5k/mo** — close, but only if every client lands at the top of every range.

To clear $10k/mo within 4–6 clients, the retainer must validate at **$1,500–$2,500/mo**, justified by: zero-credential manual-first onboarding, audit-trail reporting, and the attention queue replacing real admin hours. The pilot exists to test exactly this willingness to pay.

**Recommendation:** pilot at **$1,500 sprint + $1,000/mo**, then price client #2 off measured value, not the hypothesis sheet.
