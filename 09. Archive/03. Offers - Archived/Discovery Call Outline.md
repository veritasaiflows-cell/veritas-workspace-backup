## Purpose

Turn an interested lead into a diagnosed opportunity with a clear next step.

## Call structure

### 1. Frame the conversation
Say:
- I want to understand how you are currently managing ideas, priorities, and execution.
- If there is a real fit, I will suggest a concrete next step instead of giving you vague advice.

### 2. Let them describe the situation
Use the discovery questions to understand:
- current priorities
- current breakdowns
- current systems
- desired outcomes

### 3. Diagnose the real problem
Reflect back what you heard.
Example:
- It sounds like the real issue is not lack of ideas. It is too many competing priorities without a reliable system for ranking and execution.

### 4. Explain the solution shape
Describe the solution at a high level:
- opportunity capture
- prioritization/ranking framework
- dashboard for visibility
- weekly review rhythm
- first execution plan

Do not oversell software or AI.
Sell clarity, prioritization, and execution.

### 5. Propose the next step
Offer a concrete next move:
- a focused follow-up session
- a 1-week setup sprint
- a pilot engagement

### 6. Confirm commitment
Clarify:
- whether they want help now
- whether the problem is painful enough to act on
- what timing makes sense

## Call outcome standard

A strong discovery call ends with one of these outcomes:
- clear no-fit
- clear fit, but later
- clear fit with a defined next engagement

Do not end with vague mutual interest and no next step.
