# Research Evidence Freshness Repair Contract

## Purpose

Repair stale or missing evidence without silently rewriting alert canon.

Each repair packet must name the ticker/question, source lineage, evidence date, freshness limit, failed check, repaired artifact, remaining uncertainty, and validator result. Scheduled work may refresh derived evidence and proofs only.

If active alert canon itself appears wrong, stop with a scoped correction proposal, exact diff, source proof, rollback, and validation plan. Static levels are never silently re-derived or auto-applied.

This contract creates no action authority.
