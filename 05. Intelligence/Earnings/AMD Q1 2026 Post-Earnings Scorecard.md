## Results summary

AMD reported Q1 2026 results:

- **Revenue:** $10.253B, up **38% YoY**, roughly flat QoQ.
- **GAAP gross margin:** 53%.
- **GAAP operating income:** $1.476B.
- **GAAP diluted EPS:** $0.84.
- **Non-GAAP gross margin:** 55%.
- **Non-GAAP operating income:** $2.540B.
- **Non-GAAP diluted EPS:** $1.37.
- **Data Center revenue:** $5.8B, up **57% YoY**, driven by EPYC demand and Instinct GPU ramp.
- **Client and Gaming revenue:** $3.6B, up **23% YoY**.
- **Embedded revenue:** $873M, up **6% YoY**.
