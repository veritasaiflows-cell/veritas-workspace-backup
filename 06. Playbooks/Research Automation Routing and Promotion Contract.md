# Research Automation Routing and Attention Contract

Automation may collect evidence, validate lineage, flag freshness decay, create a candidate review packet, and route an item to research or high-attention review.

It may not promote a generated claim into canon, change static levels, or create account, order, capital, execution, or simulated-account state. A material canon correction stops at a scoped proposal with source proof, diff, rollback, and validation.
